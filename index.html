THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY YOU CAN'T SEE

Your brand doesn't stop when the phone starts ringing.

Businesses carefully manage their website, advertising, visual identity and customer service — but the phone experience is often left to default prompts, silence, generic hold music or messages that no longer reflect the business.

Ordio treats those moments as part of the brand experience. Every greeting, menu, wait state and message is an opportunity to sound clear, current and intentional.

The opportunity is simple: make the experience customers hear as considered as the one they see.

01 / GREETING

Generic system prompt

The first thing a caller hears may have nothing to do with the brand they chose to contact.

02 / WAITING

Silence or irrelevant audio

Waiting time becomes a blank space instead of useful, current communication.

03 / INFORMATION

Outdated messaging

Promotions, opening hours or service information can quickly fall out of date.
